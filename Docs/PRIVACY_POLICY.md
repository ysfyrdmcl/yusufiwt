# yusufiwt privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-07-18

yusufiwt is local-first and does not sell data, advertise, or cross-app
track. Prompt text, source, terminal output, command content, full paths,
credentials, and secret answers are not retained by default.

The optional companion account stores a one-way account-subject hash, public
device keys, device display names/roles, short-lived routing metadata, encrypted
APNs tokens, and end-to-end encrypted envelopes. The relay cannot decrypt agent
content. Acknowledged/expired ciphertext is removed under short retention;
encrypted infrastructure backups expire under the backup schedule.

Apple may provide aggregate crash/App Store metrics under Apple’s terms.
yusufiwt sends no custom content-bearing analytics stream. Support exports
require user action/consent and can be reviewed before sharing; secret/sensitive
attachments are excluded and credential shapes/home paths are redacted.

Users can delete local completed history in macOS Privacy. Companion users can
choose **Settings → Delete Account and Relay Data** to delete the live account,
devices, tokens, pairing sessions, and pending envelopes, then local credentials.
See `Docs/DATA_DELETION.md`.

For access, correction, deletion, privacy, or support requests, contact
support@agentcontrol.app. Account requests are verified before disclosure.
